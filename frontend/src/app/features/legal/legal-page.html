Privacy policy

The short version: your documents are yours, they are processed on our own servers, and they are deleted on the schedule below.

What we store

  • The files you upload, and the versions our tools produce from them. Nothing is sent to a third party for processing — every conversion, OCR and signature runs on infrastructure we operate.
  • An account, if you make one: your email address, a display name if you give one, and a hashed password.
  • A guest session, if you do not: a random token in your browser. No name, no address, no email.
  • Signing records, if you send or sign a document: who acted, when, from which IP address and with which browser. That is the point of an electronic signature — it is printed on the certificate of completion, and the disclosure says so before anybody signs.

How long

Files uploaded without an account deleted within {{ retention().guest_hours }} hours
Files in an account kept until you delete them
Documents you move to the trash deleted after {{ retention().trash_days }} days
Exports (Word, images, text) deleted after {{ retention().export_hours }} hours
Signed envelopes (the sealed PDF, the certificate and the audit trail) kept indefinitely, including after you delete your account — they are the other signers' record of the agreement
Signature requests nobody signed deleted with your account

We also keep a record of the operations you ran — which tool, when, and whether it worked — for {{ retention().job_days }} days, and then delete it. You can see that list yourself under Settings. The inputs to those operations, such as the words you searched for in a redaction, are deleted after {{ retention().job_params_days }} days.

A test asserts these are the same numbers the sweepers that delete things actually use — if one changed and this page did not, the build fails.

Advertising

ZenPDF is free and paid for by advertising. Where consent is required we ask before any ad code loads, and if you decline, none loads. Ads never appear on a page showing your document: not in the editor, not in the viewer, not in a signing ceremony, and not on the verification page.

Cookies and storage

  • zen_access / zen_refresh — your login, in browser storage. Only if you have an account.
  • zen_guest — the random token that ties your files to this browser when you have no account.
  • zen_ad_consent — your answer to the banner, so we do not ask again.
  • Advertising cookies, set by Google, only after you allow them.

Getting in touch

Settings → Your data has both: download everything we hold as a zip, or delete your account. Deleting removes your documents and their files. It does not remove signature envelopes other people have already signed — that record is their evidence of an agreement, so it is kept and detached from your account. To ask anything else, or to report misuse, write to the address in the footer of any email we send.

About ZenPDF

ZenPDF is a PDF workspace that does not ask you to sign up before it does anything. Drop a file on any tool page and it works: merge, split, compress, rotate, annotate, edit text, fill forms, OCR, convert, compare, protect, redact and sign — with no account, no watermark and no “create a free account to download”.

How it is paid for

Advertising, and nothing else. There is no subscription, no per-file charge and no upsell in the middle of a job. Ads sit in three places — the landing page, the dashboard, and the panel that appears when a job has finished — and never on a page that is showing your document.

What an account adds

A library that does not expire, larger files, more pages, a saved signature, and the ability to send documents to other people for signature. Everything else is the same either way.

How it works

Every operation runs on our own servers using open-source PDF tooling — PyMuPDF, qpdf, Ghostscript, Tesseract, pyHanko. Your files are not sent to a third-party API to be processed, and they are deleted on the schedule in the privacy policy.
